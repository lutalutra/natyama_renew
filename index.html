    About

    nat/yama is a designer based in Berlin with an editorial approach. Her work takes shape as digital products, print or graphics for spaces, often across languages and cultures, making in-depth topics graspable. After several years at a design studio in Tokyo and a period of graduate study, she now works freelance.
